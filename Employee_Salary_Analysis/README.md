# Employee Salary Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee salary data to identify key factors and patterns that influence monthly compensation.

The analysis focuses on salary differences across:
- Departments
- Job Roles
- Experience Years
- Education Levels
- Gender Distribution

The project was developed as a data analysis and visualization project to extract meaningful insights from employee salary data.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze average monthly salary across departments.
- Compare average salary by job role.
- Understand the relationship between experience and salary.
- Analyze salary differences based on education level.
- Identify the Top 10 highest-paid employees.
- Examine gender distribution across departments.
- Extract meaningful business insights from the data.

---

## 📌 Key Insights

### 1. Average Salary by Department

Finance has the highest average salary among all departments, while Marketing has the lowest, highlighting a significant salary variation across departments.

### 2. Average Salary by Job Role

Finance Manager has the highest average salary among all job roles at **$9,487 per month**, while Digital Marketer has the lowest at **$6,326**, highlighting a significant salary variation across job roles.

### 3. Average Salary by Experience

Average salary varies across experience levels, indicating that professional experience has an impact on employee compensation.

### 4. Average Salary by Education

Employees with a **High School** education have the highest average salary, followed by **Master's** and **Bachelor's** degree holders, while **PhD** employees have the lowest average salary in this dataset.

### 5. Top 10 Highest-Paid Employees

The Top 10 highest-paid employees have significantly higher salaries than the overall employee average, indicating a substantial salary gap between top earners and the rest of the workforce.

### 6. Gender Distribution by Department

Gender distribution varies across departments, with some departments having a higher proportion of male employees while others show a more balanced gender distribution.

## 💡 Overall Conclusion

Overall, the analysis shows that employee compensation varies considerably depending on department, job role, experience, and education level.

The analysis also reveals a noticeable salary gap between the highest-paid employees and the overall workforce.

These insights can help organizations better understand salary structures and identify areas that may require further investigation.
