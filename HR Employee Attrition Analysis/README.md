# HR Analytics Dashboard — Excel

An end-to-end HR data analysis project built in Microsoft Excel, covering data cleaning, exploratory analysis, PivotTables, KPI development, data visualization, and dashboard design.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an HR dataset containing **600 employees** to identify workforce patterns, employee satisfaction levels, income differences, and employee attrition trends.

The project follows a practical Data Analyst workflow:

**Data Cleaning → Exploratory Analysis → PivotTables → KPIs → Charts → Dashboard → Business Insights**

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Attrition

There are **76 employees with attrition out of 600 employees**, resulting in an overall attrition rate of approximately **12.7%**.

### 2. Workforce Distribution

**Research & Development** is the largest department with **351 employees**, representing approximately **58.5%** of the total workforce.

### 3. Attrition by Department

**Human Resources** has the highest attrition rate at approximately **16.2%**, followed by:

* Sales: **13.3%**
* Research & Development: **11.7%**

### 4. Attrition by Gender

Male employees have an attrition rate of approximately **14.9%**, compared with **9.9%** for female employees.

This represents a difference of approximately **5 percentage points**.

### 5. Education Profile

The workforce is primarily composed of employees with Bachelor's and Master's degrees:

* Bachelor: **53.8%**
* Master: **29.2%**
* High School: **11.0%**
* PhD: **6.0%**

Bachelor's and Master's degree holders together represent approximately **83%** of the workforce.

### 6. Job Satisfaction

Job Satisfaction level **3** is the most common rating, representing approximately **37.7%** of employees.

Employees with satisfaction levels **3 or 4** represent approximately **60.2%** of the workforce.

### 7. Average Income by Education

Employees with a **PhD** have the highest average monthly income at **3,682**, while employees with a High School education have the lowest average at **3,511**.

The difference between the highest and lowest groups is **171**.

### 8. Average Income by Department

Average monthly income is relatively similar across departments:

| Department             | Average Monthly Income |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------: |
| Sales                  |                  3,551 |
| Human Resources        |                  3,549 |
| Research & Development |                  3,533 |
| Overall                |                  3,540 |

The small difference between departments indicates that average income is relatively consistent across the organization.
